Questions, answered.

Is it free?

Yes, while we're piloting. Create a free account to use the full journey. We'll tell you clearly, well in advance, before anything changes.

Will Karabo make things up to make my CV look better?

No. Your CV is built only from your original CV and the facts you confirm. Every new line is labelled as from your CV, something you said, or suggested wording, and you can remove anything before you approve it.

Will my CV get past applicant tracking systems (ATS)?

Our templates are simple, text-based and use standard headings, which screening software reads well. No one can honestly guarantee that every system reads every CV perfectly, so we don't.

How is the Job Fit suitability score worked out?

Karabo turns the whole advert into requirements. Essential ones count double. A requirement your CV clearly shows earns full marks, needs clarifying earns half, and a gap earns nothing. Karabo's review can adjust the result by up to 10 points and always explains why. It shows the evidence on your CV, not whether you'll be hired.

What happens when I answer the clarifying questions?

Answers with real evidence upgrade that requirement, lift your score and go into your customised CV, highlighted so you can see them. Karabo shows how your scores moved and asks whether to add the new lines to your main CV too.

Can I just practise interviews, or check one job?

Yes. After you create your account, choose what you want to do: the full CV journey, a Job Fit check, interview practice, your LinkedIn profile or the jobs board. Returning users land straight on these options, and the Menu in Karabo's chat takes you there anytime.

Can I go back to an earlier step?

Yes. Click any step at the top. If something is missing, Karabo shows you what's needed and takes you there in one click.

Do I have to speak, or can I type?

Either. Type your answers, or speak them where your browser supports voice input.

Can I apply for jobs through Career AI?

You prepare your application here, then apply on the employer's own site. We never apply on your behalf.

Do you guarantee I'll get a job?

No one honestly can. We help you present your experience accurately and prepare well. The decision is always the employer's.

Who can see my CV and answers?

You. If your access is sponsored by a university, employer or programme, they see progress only, never your CV or answers, unless you choose to share them.

Can I delete my information?

Yes. See our Privacy Policy for how to delete your information or ask us to.
