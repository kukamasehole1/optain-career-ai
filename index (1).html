The full journey

A CV built from what you actually did.

Karabo rebuilds your CV around results, then shows you exactly what changed and why.

  • Every line is labelled: from your CV, something you said, or suggested wording.
  • Nothing is invented. No made-up numbers, employers or qualifications.
  • Ready to send: a clean, text-based PDF that screening software can read.

Know your fit before you apply.

Paste any advert. Karabo checks every duty and requirement against your CV and gives you a suitability score with the reasons behind it.

  • A score you can trust: worked out from the requirements, with every adjustment explained.
  • Four areas: experience, skills, qualifications, and seniority and industry fit.
  • Gaps become questions. Your answers lift the score and go into a customised CV.
