From your CV to a customised application.

Take the full journey, or go straight to what you need: a Job Fit check, interview practice or your LinkedIn profile. Karabo guides you either way, and you can jump to any step anytime.

Get set up

Welcome, account, choose, template and upload

Tell Karabo your name and create a free account so your work is saved. Then choose what you want to do today. Preview three templates (ATS Classic, Modern Professional and Executive) and upload your CV as a Word file, PDF or pasted text.

Diagnose

Your CV and an honest diagnostic

Your CV appears on the template in your own words, with every section kept. Beside it: five scores with reasons, your strengths, missing evidence and three priorities.

Strengthen

A role-by-role deep dive, then review

Karabo works through your last three roles: team and scope, responsibilities, results, systems, problems solved and recognition. Then you review the enhanced CV and approve it, or ask for changes.

Apply

Job Fit, then customise

Paste an advert for a suitability score. Karabo asks about anything unclear, recalculates, and builds a customised CV, introduction and bio, with your new lines highlighted.

An honest diagnostic, right beside your CV.

Clarity, measurable achievements, readability for screening software, skills evidence and completeness, each scored out of 100 with a plain explanation. The diagnostic stays in your career file. It's for you, not for the download.

Questions that find the evidence.

One question at a time, by typing or speaking. When an answer is vague, Karabo asks one follow-up for the specifics: how many, how much, what changed. Each fact appears under the role it belongs to, and you can untick anything.

See what changed, and why.

Your enhanced CV leads with results. Every new line is labelled with where it came from, and everything else is kept from your original.

A suitability score with reasons.

Essential requirements count double. Demonstrated earns full marks, needs clarifying earns half. Karabo's review can adjust the result by up to 10 points, and always says why. After you answer the clarifying questions, you see your score move, from this to this, and what shifted it.

An application ready to send.

Your customised CV puts the most relevant evidence first. Lines that came from your Job Fit answers are highlighted, so you know exactly what's new. Choose whether those lines also go into your main CV.

A prep pack and a practice interview room help you get ready for the questions you're likely to face.
