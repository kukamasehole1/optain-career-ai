Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

Last updated: 20 November 2026 · Applies to the OptAIn Career AI pilot

1. Who we are

OptAIn Career AI is a product of [Company legal name], registration number [Registration number], of [Registered address] ("OptAIn", "we", "us"). We are the responsible party for your personal information.

Our Information Officer is [Information Officer name], reachable at [Information Officer email] or [phone number].

2. What personal information we collect

  • Contact and account details: your name, email address and password.
  • CV content: everything in the CV you upload or paste, including your work history, education, skills and contact details.
  • Interview answers: what you tell Karabo, typed or spoken, and the facts you confirm.
  • Job adverts: adverts you paste or upload, and your answers to Job Fit questions.
  • What we create with you: diagnostics, enhanced and customised CVs, Job Fit Reports, introductions and bios.
  • Enquiries: details you send through our institutions form or by email.
  • Basic technical information: such as your IP address and browser type, which our hosting provider processes to deliver the site.

Please don't include special personal information, such as your ID number, health, religion or criminal record, in your CV unless a job specifically requires it. Ask before you share anyone else's details.

3. Why we use it, and our lawful basis

We use your personal information only to provide the service you ask for: to move your CV onto a template, diagnose it, interview you, build your CVs and reports, check your fit for a job and prepare your application. We also use it to respond to enquiries and to keep the service secure and working.

Our lawful basis is your consent, which you give when you create your account, and the need to perform the service you asked for (POPIA section 11(1)(a) and (b)). You can withdraw consent at any time. This doesn't affect processing that already happened.

We do not sell your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

4. AI processing by a third-party provider

To analyse your CV and write with you, we send the relevant content (your CV, answers and job adverts) to our AI provider, Anthropic (Claude AI models), through our secure server. The provider processes it to return a result to us, under terms that restrict how they may use it.

AI can make mistakes. That's why every new line is labelled with its source and nothing goes on your CV until you approve it. Scores are guidance, not decisions about you. No one is hired or rejected by our system.

5. Who we share it with, and cross-border transfers

We share personal information only with operators who help us run the service: our AI provider and our hosting provider (Netlify). Our fonts are hosted on our own site, so no third-party font service sees your visits.

Some of these providers process information outside South Africa. We transfer it only where the recipient is bound by law, binding agreements or rules that give protection substantially similar to POPIA, or where you have consented, as POPIA section 72 requires.

If a university, employer or programme sponsors your access, they see progress information only. They never see your CV or answers unless you choose to share them.

6. How long we keep it

During the pilot, your account and career file (your CV, diagnostic, answers and CVs) are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. Content sent for analysis is processed to return your result and is not kept by us longer than needed. Enquiries are kept for up to [e.g. 24 months].

You can clear your career file at any time with Start over in the app, or by clearing your browser's site data. When we launch accounts stored on our servers, we'll update this policy and tell you first.

7. Your rights

Under POPIA you have the right to:

  • Access: ask whether we hold personal information about you, and for a copy of it.
  • Correction: ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, out of date or incomplete.
  • Deletion: ask us to delete information we no longer need or may not lawfully keep.
  • Objection: object to processing on reasonable grounds, and withdraw your consent.
  • Complain: lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.

To use these rights, email our Information Officer at [Information Officer email]. We may ask you to confirm your identity, and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

8. Cookies and local storage

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. The app uses your browser's local storage to save your progress and career file on your device, so you can pick up where you left off. This is necessary for the service to work. Clearing your browser data removes it.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and limited access. If we become aware of a security compromise affecting your information, we'll notify you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

10. Questions and complaints

Please contact our Information Officer first, at [Information Officer email]. If you're not satisfied, you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa):

  • Website: inforegulator.org.za (complaints can be lodged online)
  • Email: enquiries@inforegulator.org.za
  • Toll free: 0800 017 160

We may update this policy as the service develops. We'll show the new date at the top and tell you about significant changes.
